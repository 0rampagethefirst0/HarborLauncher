# Release notes

## 0.14.2 — 2026-11-02

- Scroll animations keep their final offset. Mods refreshes and watched file changes preserve the viewport, selection and grid page.
- Dock controls use Layout, Style and Behavior. Taskbar controls use Style, Interaction and Apps, with advanced settings tucked away.
- Transparency supports 0.5–99.5% in half-percent steps; activation holds can be 0.5 seconds.
- All five themes expose ten color roles, including wallpaper, logo and fire. Startup and shared artwork follow the palette.
- Tokyo Dragon breathes upward at a 17-degree side angle. Filled flame tongues morph into Home's rounded surface before its contents reveal, and closing restores the saved desktop rotation.
- Dragon playback prepares 160 materialized frames in the background, avoids repeated card layout and uses a fading panel-colored curtain for the final reveal.
- Bundled definitions are 1.8.2, with 57 packages and 90 FPS recorded previews.

Validation reports are included with the release. Offscreen rendering and animation-update timings do not establish a guaranteed display frame rate or a complete manual UI pass.
